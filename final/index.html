Faces of Data

Comparing published enrollment demographics with brochure imagery representation

About the data

This project analyzes demographic representation using two primary sources from 1999–2019: Davidson College Admission Brochures and the Institutional Factfile. To focus on racial representation, we utilized RStudio to consolidate gender-specific data into three unified categories: White, Black, and Other Race. All raw counts were converted into integers to ensure precise proportional calculations. No AI tools were used in the data cleaning or restructuring process.

For the 2003–2004 academic year, while enrollment data was available in the Factfile, the corresponding admission brochure was missing. To maintain transparency, we displayed the Factfile data while representing the brochure panel with a neutral grey grid. This visual choice ensures consistency with our interactive hover effects while clearly signaling the absence of marketing materials for that specific period.
